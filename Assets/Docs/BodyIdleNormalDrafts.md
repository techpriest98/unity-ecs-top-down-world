# Body Idle normal-map draft layers

These are editable art drafts, not production-validated lighting data. Player rendering is unchanged. Original colour art and the first normal draft are preserved.

- `Art/Player/Animations/Body/body_idle_normal_cloth_v2.png`: cloth-only alpha; shoulder mask starts at local row 34 instead of 39. Reuses the prior built-in imagegen cloth proposal, without another generation.
- `Art/Player/Animations/Body/body_idle_normal_skin_draft.png`: separate built-in imagegen skin proposal, selected through a source-colour material mask. Hair, beard, brows, eyes and black details are excluded.

Both sheets are 128x512. Transparent texels also contain palette RGBs; do not interpret their RGB without alpha. Import as linear RGB data, point sampled, uncompressed, not Unity packed tangent-space normal textures. Combine layers over a neutral normal base, preserving original sprite alpha and separately handling unlit outlines.

`PrepareBodyNormalDraft.ps1 -Layer Cloth|Skin` registers each generated silhouette bounding box to its source frame, samples it, and snaps decoded vectors to the exact current block-editor picker. This is approximate registration, not recovery of exact original fold/nose positions. Source-colour masks are heuristic, so inspect skin boundaries and shoulder pixels in a pixel editor.

Validation: 7,358 visible cloth pixels; 2,722 skin pixels; 238 cloth pixels in restored shoulder rows 34-38. No material-mask overlap, no alpha beyond original, and every RGB belongs to the 12-colour picker. These checks do not validate artistic normal directions or Unity rendering.

## Skin generation prompt (built-in imagegen)

Use case precise-object-edit. Technical pixel-art normal map of supplied body_idle 128x512 sprite sheet. Edit target is this exact sheet: 8 character sprites in 2 columns x4 rows (front, back, left, right; two idle frames each). Keep all 8 poses, all silhouettes, layout, proportions, margins, canvas aspect and per-sprite alignment EXACT. No new sprites or limbs. Only change surface colors to encoded NORMALS, not lit skin artwork. This pass focuses entirely on exposed SKIN: face forehead cheeks nose ear neck calves knees ankles feet, and skin parts already visible. No arms are present separately in this sheet: do not invent arms. Original skin light painted left-to-right means brightness is shape, not pigmentation. Make round leg/calves and toes, smoothly shaped brow forehead and cheek planes, projecting nose with bridge/top/side/underside, neck cylindrical volume. Hair/beard/eyebrows/eyes are different dark materials: do not treat their albedo darkness as depressions or bumps, set these plus black outlines and all clothing to neutral #8080FF. Pixel-art faceted flat normal color regions, strictly no anti-aliasing or gradients. Mandatory ONLY 12 RGB palette colors: #36E880 #80FF80 #C9E880 (up left/center/right); #25C0BF #80DAD9 #DAC0BF (45deg up left/center/right); #3680E8 #8080FF #C980E8 (vertical left/center/right); #3636C9 #8025DA #C936C9 (down45 left/center/right). This is world-oriented block-editor palette: normal x left/right, y up, z toward viewer. Do not substitute conventional arbitrary tangent-space colors. Transparent background. No text, checkerboard, floor, shadows, perspective or labels. Cover entire exposed skin of all 8 sprites, not just torso. Preserve neutral opaque silhouettes around unprocessed hair/clothing so each sprite remains registered for extracting skin later.
